# Continue, wait and finish truthfully

## Resume the authorized work

On continuation, discover streams and catch up on the relevant discussion,
checklist changes and Eric's replies. A coordinator considers incoming material
across the project and checks actual worker task states through the host.
Retain separate committed cursors for each stream; incremental change reads
and bounded waits avoid rereading settled history. A quiet response says only
that no committed change was observed within that wait.

Keep the shared in-scope checklist as the continuing work obligation. Add
Eric's subsequent in-scope requests and necessary completion work. A local
plan or milestone is a subset; completing it does not discharge unfinished
required work. Future ideas remain feed proposals until accepted. Resolve
material incoming questions from evidence, assign authorized changes, explain
a rejection or leave the decision needed from Eric visible. Reading a question
is not its disposition.

Reconcile the current outcome with any native goal when direction changes.
An absent or completed goal does not cancel ordinary authorized work. Create
a goal only with explicit user authority; use supported lifecycle tools and
report a mismatch rather than assume its objective can be rewritten. A goal
is not a subscription to shared-state changes.

## Choose the wait that fits

- **A running process or worker:** use a finite model-free wait within the
  server's advertised limit. Keep other feasible work moving. Check the outcome
  and relevant streams between waits, rotating them when necessary. A timeout
  does not prove a worker stopped or make the outcome blocked. Inspect its
  actual host handle before restarting it.
- **An answer needed overnight:** record the question, affected work, responsible
  owner, resumption condition and actual way to continue. Continue unaffected
  authorized work. End the active watch at its checkpoint; do not spend model
  turns polling for the answer overnight.
- **A recurring responsibility:** each authorized check records its result,
  handled and open items, next expected action and who or what will run it.
  Finishing a check does not complete the standing responsibility. Use an
  arranged future trigger only when authorized and actually available; record
  a need for explicit continuation otherwise. Keep no worker active between
  checks merely to preserve context.

A feed post does not start or resume a stopped agent. If a worker has no active
watch or supported continuation, the coordinator must explicitly continue its
host task for a revision. Record what will resume work and who owns that action;
do not promise automatic pickup. Creating a schedule is a separate authorized
host action, not an Agent Mail operation.

## End a turn or close an outcome

A worker may return at its finite checkpoint with review pending. Its item
remains unfinished; the coordinator retains responsibility for the next action.
A blocker leaves the obligation unfinished too. Advance other feasible work.
An incomplete return needs the exact dependency, owner and resumption path
when no further authorized progress is feasible. A turn ending, a quiet wait,
a submitted result or a blocked label is not completion.

Report a bounded outcome complete only after the required in-scope checklist
work is verified and integrated and material incoming items have dispositions.
Closing a failed attempt does not satisfy its unmet requirement. Chat archiving
is optional housekeeping and does not gate completion. Collect each contribution
and its acceptance or closure; retain workers still needed for revisions or
collaboration. When archiving is used, record it in Agent Mail only after the
actual host action: the archival field neither performs nor verifies that action.
Clean up temporary task-owned servers, watchers and processes unless still in
active use, preserving shared or operational services. Preserve the shared
database while collaboration or review still uses it.
