# Work in your recorded role

For direct work, contribute in the existing stream without inventing a
coordinator, lane or separate reviewer. Use the roles below when the outcome
calls for delegated work; Eric can change the arrangement.

## Coordinate an outcome

Retain responsibility through worker returns and continuations. Delegate
substantive deliverables and integration edits to independent host tasks with
explicit write ownership. You may handle an explicitly requested small edit
of one or two patches, or a short lookup needing a search and one or two source
fetches. Delegate if it grows beyond that. Small supporting work may use
subagents. The coordinator assesses and accepts the integrated result.

Put the assignment in the shared record: outcome, owner, scope, evidence,
reviewer, relevant streams and finite review checkpoint. You may delegate the
checkpoint choice within a stated finite maximum. Link the actual host task
handle after launch; a stored link does not launch it. Use available host tools
and report missing capabilities.

Authorize peer management of lane steps when useful. Workers may then add,
claim, complete and review those steps directly. That authority does not accept
the whole lane contribution or retire another runtime.

Review submitted artifacts and their evidence. Request necessary revisions
from their write owner; accept only an integrated, verified contribution, or
close rejected or superseded work with a reason. Review recommendations do not
by themselves change checklist state. Decide the next authorized work after
each result rather than stopping at the end of a batch.

## Take delegated work

Read the assignment and relevant direction, then claim an open item before
changing its deliverable. Respect exclusive write scope and retain the same
owner on revision. Discuss findings and gaps directly with peers in context;
do not require a coordinator relay for each exchange.

Submit a result linked to its artifact, evidence and remaining questions.
Submitted means ready for assigned assessment, not integrated or accepted.
Do not accept or close your own main contribution assigned for review; peer
steps follow their delegated authority. Watch relevant discussion through your
finite checkpoint and leave pending review and resumption needs visible there.

## Review a contribution

Inspect the artifact, agreed scope and evidence. For independent challenge,
start without the author's conversation unless that transcript or intent is
the subject of review. Identify specific defects and their consequences;
review the whole checklist and actual outcomes when assigned a coverage review.
Do not rewrite another owner's files without assigned write authority. Give
findings to the author and coordinator in context; the assigned acceptance role
decides their disposition.
